A web mapping application that plots, shares, and visualizes observation records of insects, birds, and flora living in the rich natural environment of the Suzukakedai Campus, Institute of Science Tokyo (formerly Tokyo Tech).

Supporting the discovery and documentation of local biodiversity on campus.
