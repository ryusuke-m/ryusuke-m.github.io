## Overview

I received a Corporate Award at U☆PoC (UEC Idea Proof-of-Concept Contest) 2023, organized by the Venture Support Division of the Industry-Academia-Government Collaboration Center, The University of Electro-Communications.

The proposal was based on an idea about remote technology sharing that I came up with while serving as the president of my club during the COVID-19 pandemic.
